# Signo

Signo gives you AI agents that watch your DeFi positions around the clock. Each agent follows a position or a market condition you choose, alerts you on Telegram when it matters, and prepares the next step for you to review and sign in your own wallet. Signo never holds your funds.

In early access, agents can also act on their own within limits you sign onchain and can revoke at any time. The contract that enforces those limits is open source: [signo-shield](https://github.com/FlipLabsAI/signo-shield).
